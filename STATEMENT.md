# QUIZ/EXAM APPLICATION WITH PERFORMANCE ANALYTICS

## Problem Statement
Students and instructors often lack a simple and easy method to create digital tests and quizzes. Already existing software is either too complex to use, not affordable, is heavy weight or do not provide a meaningful performance analytics for the students and the teachers to help the student in there weak areas. This projects aims to solve this problem and provide a solution that is easy to operate, free, light-weight and gives satisfactory analytics.

## Scope of the project
- Creation and management of a categorized question bank (add, edit, delete, filter by topic/difficulty)
- Randomized or category based quiz generation and administration via a command-line interface
- Automated scoring and grading of quiz attempts 
- Persistent storage of questions and score history
- Analytics on quiz performance across multiple attempts (average score, topic-wise weak areas, trends)

## Target Users
- **Students / Learners** take quizzes for self-practice and review their performance
- **Admin / Teacher** creates and manages the question bank, and reviews performance analytics

## High-Level Features
1. **Question Bank Management**- add, edit, delete, and categorize quiz questions by topic and difficulty
2. **Quiz Engine**- generates and administers quizzes (random selection or by topic), collects and validates user answers
3. **Automated Scoring**- grades attempts, computes percentage score, pass/fail status, and per-question correctness
4. **Score Analytics & Reporting**- tracks past performance, identifies weak topics, shows improvement trends, and generates exportable reports
